How I work

Hands-on

I read the code. At Oracle I peer-reviewed pull requests on the identity and access-certification paths and debugged production authentication incidents with the on-call rotation. At MYOB I wrote the first prototypes of the observability and security automation platforms, then handed them to the teams that own them now.

Turnarounds

I took over an S3 team after a 17-month leadership gap — twelve competing workstreams and flat engagement. We reprioritized with stakeholders, paired senior engineers with junior ones, and cut the workstream count. Engagement up 26%; 87% of the mid-year roadmap delivered.

Growing people

Engagement from 63% to 87% at MYOB while the team went from 5 to 50. At Amazon, three SDE1s promoted to SDE2 and two SDE2s to SDE3 in two years; at Oracle, two engineers into tech-lead roles. I teach managers to review code themselves, so the bar holds when I am not in the room.

Day to day: Python, Go, TypeScript, SQL, AWS, Postgres and DynamoDB. Twenty years of the rest — Java, C#, Oracle, Db2, Cassandra, Kafka — is on the résumé rather than here.
